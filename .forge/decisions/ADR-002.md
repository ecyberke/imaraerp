# ADR-002 — M-Pesa callback trust model
Status: proposed · Supersedes: architecture v21 §1.1 "signature/HMAC verification plus replay protection (timestamp + nonce)" for Daraja only.
## Context
Daraja STK callbacks are unsigned; the provider cannot meet §1.1. v1.0 correlates callbacks through an app-generated opaque ID and settles the invoice from the callback body.
## Decision
The callback is a **claim**. (1) Path carries `HMAC-SHA256(MPESA_CALLBACK_HMAC_KEY, stk_request_id)`; mismatch → 404 with no body. (2) Source IP must be in `MPESA_ALLOWED_IPS` (behind a trusted proxy header config). (3) CheckoutRequestID must match a `pending_external` row for that tenant. (4) The callback is stored, then a Daraja **STK Query** must confirm ResultCode 0, amount and MSISDN before a Payment posts. (5) Unique constraints on CheckoutRequestID and receipt number. (6) Reconciliation job queries anything pending > 3 min; expire at 24 h.
## Consequences
Settlement gains ~1–3 s latency. A forged callback can, at worst, trigger a status query. Requires confirmation (ASM-003): this is payment behaviour.
