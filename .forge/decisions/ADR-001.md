# ADR-001 — Govern Imara ERP with AI Web App Forge from v1.1.0 (brownfield), risk class R4
Status: accepted 2026-10-02, approved by Lavyd · Supersedes: execution_plan.md as the source of ordering and exit criteria for work after v1.0.
## Context
v1.0 was built from a prose architecture (v21) and a branch plan. Traceability, gates and evidence were maintained by discipline, not by tooling, and drifted in places (audit observer coverage, §1.1 webhook HMAC promise, `assertEqualsWithDelta` guidance after money became integer cents, stale README).
## Decision
`forge init --mode brownfield`; `.forge/*.yaml` becomes authoritative for requirements, tasks, tests and gates; the v21 document remains authoritative for the **domain model and posting rules** and is referenced, not copied. Declare `risk_class: R4` (financial ledger, payroll PII, payment collection, statutory tax): R3 gate list plus no project-scoped exceptions on security-adjacent gates and no `not_applicable` on eight self-audit categories.
## Consequences
Every PR after v1.0 links a TASK-*. Accessibility, performance, reliability and adversarial become required gates. Blocked integration work can only be deferred with RC-scoped, expiring exceptions.
