# ADR-007 — Purchase Order cancellation and short-close
Status: proposed (ASM-008).
## Decision
`cancelled` allowed from pending_approval, approved, ordered with zero received quantity; releases RM reservations and any open DemandTrigger link; no ledger posting. `short_closed` from partially_received: unreceived line quantities cancelled, received lines and their postings stand. Both require a reason and are audited; cancelling an approved PO above the approval threshold needs the same maker-checker as approval. `Project.cancel()` then resolves open POs instead of flagging them.
